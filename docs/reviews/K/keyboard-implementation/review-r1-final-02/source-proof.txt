K115-06: KeyboardDecoder.batch obtains arrays for KeyboardBatch then replaces both keys and issues fields with fresh ArrayLists; returned arrays are copied by KeyboardBatch. Decoder-held batch-output capacities therefore do not retain the previous feed's output size. appendPaste replaces the paste field with a fresh ArrayList immediately upon exceeding maxPasteBytes, before reporting the issue. finish and successful/end-invalid paste paths also replace the payload list. sequence and endPrefix are bounded by maxSequenceBytes (plus the fixed paste marker candidate) and five pending terminator bytes respectively. SDK1.2 clear retains ArrayList capacity, as independently documented in keyboard-review/round-01/arraylist-clear.disasm; replacement removes the decoder's reference to the old backing store, subject to normal GC.
ESC C2/maxSequenceBytes=1 source trace: ESC initializes start=0 and state=1; C2 sets utfAlt/utfC1Candidate and utfRemaining without prematurely reporting a sequence overrun because it may become a new encoded C1. A real timeout enters pendingC1OverBudget, sets drained, emits SequenceTooLong@0 once, and waitingIdle becomes false. A subsequent 9B completes U+009B and takes the explicit utfC1Candidate C1 branch before checking drained; c1 starts the new CSI at the C2 offset, sets its two-byte raw introducer and applies the configured budget. With limit1 the new CSI itself reports SequenceTooLong at offset1 and drains, rather than inheriting the prior ESC's state. finish before C1 completion reports the old SequenceTooLong once instead of IncompleteInput. If C1 completes before timeout there is no old ESC overrun notification; the C1 uses its own raw two-byte budget and offset. This states the selected engineering ruling and source logic, not measured behavior.
UTF8 sequence-drain source trace: an E0 lead starts sequence UTF8 tracking after MalformedSequence and latches drain; even if two continuation bytes produce a numeric value in U+0080..U+009F via an overlong representation, utfCode < utfMin rejects it as C1, leaving the sequence in drain. Only a complete valid encoded C1 (C2 plus its continuation) may invoke c1 at that boundary. This is source reasoning, not a new runtime vector.
